## Round 4 · June 2026: The Optimizer
- Archive name previously shown on the Hub: "The Optimizer 1.0"
- opensAt: Mon 1 Jun 2026, 10:00 WAT
- closesAt: Wed 17 Jun 2026, 23:59 WAT
- summary: Identify a real-world bottleneck in your department and use AI to architect a repeatable solution.

**Brief:**
> Every department has that one manual task that takes a bit too long, creates a bottleneck, or slows down daily delivery. This month, we are shifting our focus from design and aesthetics straight into core business operations and problem-solving!
>
> Your mission for the June AI Challenge is simple: Identify a real-world bottleneck in your department and use AI to architect a solution.
>
> Whether you are in Sales, Tech, HR, or Operations, this is your chance to use smart prompt engineering, data tools, or workflow automations to save time, eliminate errors, and optimize how your team works.
>
> **How to participate**
> 1. Identify the Bottleneck: Review the daily tasks within your department and pinpoint a manual process, delay, or operational bottleneck that needs optimization.
> 2. Use AI to build a repeatable solution. Your deliverable can be a functional app prototype, a PDF document detailing your master prompts and outputs, or a short video demonstrating a multi-step workflow. We welcome other formats as well, provided they effectively demonstrate your solution's value and functionality.
> 3. Submit Your Entry: Ensure your solution, prompts, or recordings are hosted via a public, shareable link, and submit it directly to the Hub for evaluation.
