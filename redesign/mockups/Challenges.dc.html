01

Why this challenge

This is our last challenge of 2026, and it ends the year on something real: your design could become the card every Descasio client and partner receives this season.

Seen by everyone who works with us. The winning card is sent to clients and partners and shared on our channels from 1 December to mid-January.
A thank-you, not a sales pitch. The card's job is to show appreciation for being part of our 2026.
A showcase of what we do. An interactive card built with AI is itself a small proof of Descasio's AI-first year.

Your call

LayoutA traditional fold-out card (cover, inside, back), a single page, a scroll story, a flip card, an animated reveal: any format that feels like a card.
ImagesBeyond Mesca, you may use other Descasio images, such as team photos from our official social media pages.
WordsWrite your own message. Keep it warm, clear and professional enough for clients.
ExtrasMusic or sound (off by default), animation, personalisation, a GIF version: anything that makes it memorable.
